## 📖 Overview

The **BIOL 2401 A&P 1 NotebookLM SLO Manager** is an educational platform designed for Human Anatomy & Physiology I students. It coordinates the equitable distribution and tracking of Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) mapped directly to official **HAPS (Human Anatomy & Physiology Society)** nominal standards.

Students use **Google NotebookLM** to ingest course textbooks (OpenStax A&P) and lecture notes to produce **5 core active-learning deliverables**:
1. **Audio**: 2-host conversational podcast deep-dive debating physiological mechanisms.
2. **Video (Shorts)**: 60-second micro-lecture script & visual storyboard breakdown.
3. **Slide Deck**: 6–8 structured presentation slides with anatomical relationships and step-by-step pathways.
4. **Infographic**: 1-page visual concept map and system summary diagram.
5. **Flashcard Set**: 10–15 high-yield active recall cards with clinical rationales.

For the **Final Exam**, students build interactive learning games via **Google AI Studio** for synthesis and extra credit.

## 🎯 Exam & Chapter Distribution (Chapters 1–12)

- **LE1 (Lecture Exam 1)**: Chapters 1, 2, 3 *(Introduction to A&P, Chemical Level of Organization, Cellular Level)* — 28 SLOs
- **LE2 (Lecture Exam 2)**: Chapters 4, 5, 6 *(Cellular Metabolism, Tissues & Histology, Integumentary System)* — 26 SLOs
- **LE3 (Lecture Exam 3)**: Chapters 7, 8, 9 *(Bone Tissue & Skeletal System, Joints & Articulations, Muscle Tissue & Muscular System)* — 34 SLOs
- **LE4 (Lecture Exam 4)**: Chapters 10, 11, 12 *(Nervous System Overview, Central & Peripheral Nervous System, General & Special Senses)* — 39 SLOs
- **Final Exam**: Chapters 1–12 Comprehensive Synthesis *(Integrative Homeostasis & Google AI Studio Games)* — 12 SLOs
